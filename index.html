CHRONOLOGICAL AGE AT MEASUREMENT
Methods

Plasma quantities were log transformed, normalized by sample median, imputed, and standardized. The combined model joins plasma and phenotype branches of a neural network. The phenotype model blends boosted trees and ridge regression. Mice were split across training, validation, and test sets.

Intervals shown above use 3,000 bootstrap resamples of held-out mice (seed 42). Bias is mean predicted minus observed age.
